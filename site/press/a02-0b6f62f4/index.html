A02 // LOST IN TIME

The Clive is a rock band from Biel/Bienne. A02 // LOST IN TIME is the first single from our third album, due in 2027 on Zwicky Records. We recorded it in Liverpool with producer James Mottershead (The Kooks, Dinosaur Pile-Up, Marmozets). It is out on all platforms on Friday 13 November 2026, with a video filmed at the Sanetsch Pass.

Embargo until 13 November 2026. Please do not share this page, the track or the video before the release.
