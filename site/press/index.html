Press

The Clive is a four-piece rock band from Biel/Bienne, Switzerland. A new album is incoming on Zwicky Records, produced by James Mottershead at Kempston Street Studio in Liverpool and mastered by Kevin Tuffy.

The first single, A02 // Lost In Time, comes out on 13 November 2026 with a video directed by Pierre Daendliker and filmed at the Sanetsch.
